About the project

Learning Mandarin, one phrase at a time

Mandarin Mate is a self-paced learning resource for people beginning or extending their Mandarin Chinese vocabulary.

How the learning pathway works

  • Beginner: greetings, introductions, numbers and basic expressions.
  • Intermediate: everyday situations such as ordering, directions and asking for help.
  • Advanced: longer sentences, opinions, plans and more complex expressions.

Each phrase is presented with simplified Chinese characters, pinyin and an English meaning.

Using the audio

The Listen buttons use your browser's built-in speech synthesis, where a Chinese voice is installed and available. This is not a substitute for listening to a range of fluent speakers or receiving feedback on spoken tones.

Learning responsibly

Translations and examples are learning aids. For formal study, important communication or nuanced language questions, check examples with a qualified teacher or fluent speaker.

Accessibility and privacy

The website is designed to work as a standalone HTML file. Core lessons, practice, quizzes and the guided tutor do not require an account or an internet connection. Progress is stored locally in your browser.

Technology

Mandarin Mate uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Its guided tutor is rules-based and works offline. A generative AI tutor can be added through a separately hosted secure backend, but this standalone release does not pretend that a live AI service is connected.
